🔒 Your Privacy, Explained

Here's exactly what AURA collects and what it never touches:

  • Your anonymized risk score is shared with the AURA research team (as a random ID like IND-XXXX)
  • Health indicators (BMI range, cycle regularity, symptom flags) help improve the AI model
  • Your name, if entered, is stored only on this device and never transmitted
  • Your email, phone, location, or any contact info is never collected
  • Your data is never sold to third parties or advertisers
  • Your device data or browser identity is never accessed
✦ Data is processed under GDPR-aligned practices. You can delete all locally stored data at any time from Settings → Clear All Saved Data.

Where Your Data Actually Goes

Saved locally in your browser (localStorage) — only accessible on this device
When you click "Share Results": anonymized stats are written directly to the AURA team's private Google Sheet (rows appear live)
Used only to improve AURA's AI model accuracy
Never stored in a public server, sold, or shared with anyone else
Never linked back to you — only your anonymous ID (e.g. IND-2026-XK9QA) is transmitted

📋 Google Sheets Setup Guide

5 steps — takes about 3 minutes. Free, no credit card needed.

Step 1 — Create a Google Sheet
Go to sheets.google.com → click Blank spreadsheet → name it "AURA PRO Data"
Step 2 — Open Apps Script
In the Sheet: click Extensions → Apps Script. A new tab opens with a code editor.
Step 3 — Paste the Script
Delete everything in the editor → paste the contents of AURA_sheets_script.gs → change your@email.com to your email → click 💾 Save.
Step 4 — Deploy as Web App
Click Deploy → New deployment
• Type: Web app
• Execute as: Me
• Who has access: Anyone
→ Click Deploy → copy the Web App URL
Step 5 — Paste URL in AURA Settings
Go to Settings → Individual Mode → paste the URL → click Test Endpoint → you should see a test row appear in your Sheet!
✅ After setup: every individual assessment auto-sends a new row to your Google Sheet. High-risk submissions also send you an email alert.
